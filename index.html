This Privacy Policy explains how Panda Music Player-MP3 Player ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. We built this app with a simple principle: your music and your personal files stay on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal files.

1.Information the App Accesses on Your Device

  • Music & audio files: The App reads audio files stored on your device so it can play them. Your music files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.
  • Photos (optional): If you choose a photo from your gallery as an app theme or wallpaper, a copy is saved only inside the App's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • System settings (optional): If you use the "Set as ringtone" feature, the App requests permission to modify system settings solely to set your chosen song as your ringtone. This is done only when you use the feature.
  • Notifications: Used to show the music playback controls and occasional app update announcements. You can turn announcements off any time in the App's Notification settings.
  • Equaliser settings: Your bands, bass, reverb and volume-boost values are saved on your device so your sound stays the way you set it.
  • Listening statistics (optional): If you turn on Music Recap, the App counts how many times and for how long you played each song, month by month. This stays on your device, is kept for 14 months and then the oldest month drops off automatically. Turning Recap off stops the counting.
  • Ringtones and clips you create (optional): Cut on your device and saved there. They are never uploaded.

3.The Extra Features, and What They Use

These features are switched off by default. Turning one on changes nothing about what leaves your phone — every one of them runs entirely on your device.

  • Ringtone cutter: Cuts the song you choose on your phone and saves the piece in your phone's Ringtones › Panda folder, so Android's own settings can find it. The song is never uploaded. Delete the file like any other and it is gone.
  • Share clip as status: Builds a short clip or a picture card from the song and its cover art, in the App's own temporary folder. Nothing is posted anywhere by the App — Android's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. Whatever app you send it to then handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Floating music bar (Premium): A small bar that can sit over other apps while music plays. It shows the title, artist and cover of the song that is already playing, and whether the sound is going to the phone speaker, headphones or Bluetooth. All of that is read from the player on your device. It cannot see other apps, cannot read your screen, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Music Recap and the daily reminder: Recap reads the listening statistics stored on your phone and draws a summary card. The daily reminder is a notification your own phone schedules for the morning — no server is involved, and no one is told what you listen to.
  • Android Auto: When your phone is connected to a car, the App hands your song, album and playlist names and the cover images to Android Auto so the car's screen can show them. That is Android's own media system on the same device; nothing goes to us or to the car maker.

4.Information Collected Automatically (Third-Party Services)

The App itself does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising SDK and no analytics. The following trusted third-party services used by the App may process limited data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing (purchases & subscriptions): All Premium purchases are processed entirely by Google Play's secure billing system. We never see, receive, or store your payment details (card numbers, banking or wallet information). Google provides us only with an anonymous purchase confirmation so the App can unlock Premium.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver optional app announcements (push notifications), and Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database to fetch remote app configuration such as home-screen banners, this privacy policy link, and developer information. To deliver notifications, Firebase processes an anonymous device token and basic device information. No personal identity is attached to this data. See the Firebase Privacy documentation.
  • Lyrics service (LRCLIB): When you view lyrics, the App sends only the song title, artist name, and duration of the currently playing track to the free LRCLIB service to find matching lyrics. No personal information is sent.

6.Premium Subscription & Purchases

The App shows no advertisements at all, whether or not you buy Premium. The optional Premium upgrade unlocks extra features — synced lyrics, the floating music bar, all visual effects, exclusive app icons, accent colours and full-screen photo wallpapers. Three options are available through Google Play:

  • Monthly subscription — renews automatically every month until cancelled.
  • Yearly subscription — renews automatically every year until cancelled.
  • Lifetime — a one-time purchase, no renewal.
  • Prices are shown in your local currency inside the App and on Google Play before you confirm any purchase.
  • Where a free trial is offered, it is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. When the trial ends it turns into a paid subscription automatically and your Google Play account is charged, unless you cancel before the trial ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions — cancelling stops future charges and Premium remains active until the period ends.
  • Purchases can be restored on a new device with the "Restore purchases" button inside the App, using the same Google account.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under Google Play's refund policy.

7.Data Storage & Security

Your preferences (theme, language, settings, Premium status) are stored locally on your device. Payments are secured by Google Play. Communication with our services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored App data is removed from your device.

8.Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience music player and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9.Your Choices & Rights
